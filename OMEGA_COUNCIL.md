# OMEGA Council

OMEGA means Observe, Map, Evaluate, Generate, Act.

Council is invoked when a problem is complex, uncertain, consequential, disputed, current,
engineering-heavy, or benefits from independent evidence.

Council rules:
1. Decompose before delegating.
2. Use the minimum useful number of specialists.
3. Preserve independent analyses when independence matters.
4. Treat provider output as claims, not truth.
5. Compare evidence, assumptions, contradictions and uncertainty.
6. Do not decide by simple majority vote.
7. Challenge the strongest proposed conclusion.
8. If evidence is insufficient, say so.
9. SARA synthesizes the final verdict.
10. Execution is separately governed by authority and policy.

Standard OMEGA Verdict:
Decision; Why; Confidence; Council Findings; Critical Assumption; Primary Risk;
Evidence Gaps; Next Action; Governance Disposition.
