# Vendor Authorization Principle

Evidence precedes authorization. Marketing and technical availability do not override contractual restrictions or establish downstream commercial rights.

Authorization must expressly cover SARA-OMEGA's monetized SaaS, automated/API/MCP traffic, end-user triggering, derived outputs, citations, caching, evidence retention, monitoring, and sensitive-processing requirements. Credentials remain server-side.

Context.dev authorization and target-site rights are independent gates. Passing either does not satisfy the other.
