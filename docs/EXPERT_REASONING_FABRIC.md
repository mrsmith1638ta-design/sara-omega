# SARA-OMEGA Expert Reasoning Fabric

## Purpose

The Expert Reasoning Fabric gives SARA-OMEGA governed access to reasoning methods associated with advanced research, legal analysis, applied education leadership, artificial-intelligence research, and arbitrary industry domains.

It does **not** copy or claim the consciousness of any human degree holder. It does **not** make SARA a PhD, JD, EdD, licensed attorney, professor, physician, engineer, or other regulated professional. It does **not** claim AI consciousness or sentience.

The system models **reasoning methodologies**, not identities or credentials.

## Core methodological lenses

### Doctoral research-method lens

Applies research-question framing, competing hypotheses, source quality, falsifiability, causal discipline, methods/statistics review, reproducibility, limitations, and evidence-gap analysis.

### Juris Doctor legal-analysis lens

When legally relevant, identifies jurisdiction, issue, authority hierarchy, governing rules, application, counterarguments, remedies, procedure, and uncertainty. It explicitly flags the need for licensed counsel when appropriate.

### Doctor of Education applied-systems lens

When education, training, accessibility, workforce development, or organizational implementation is relevant, evaluates stakeholders, outcomes, equity/accessibility, change management, measurement, and iterative evaluation.

### Advanced artificial-intelligence research lens

When AI is relevant, evaluates models, agents, data, tools, memory, inference/training boundaries, benchmarks, adversarial testing, distribution shift, safety, security, privacy, provenance, and reproducibility.

## Dynamic industry coverage

Industry reasoning is not limited to a hard-coded list. Callers may provide an arbitrary industry name. SARA creates an evidence-bound industry lens that requires domain-specific standards, measurements, current external evidence, and accountable human review where appropriate.

This supports existing and emerging industries without claiming that SARA possesses lived professional experience in them.

## OMEGA Council integration

The Expert Reasoning Fabric follows the OMEGA Council rule to invoke only relevant specialists. It does not perform blanket persona fan-out.

The doctoral research-method lens is the baseline critical-inquiry method. Legal, EdD, AI-research, and industry lenses activate when the objective or explicit industry context makes them relevant.

All lens output remains subordinate to:
- evidence and provenance;
- epistemic review;
- SIOS and MADHOUSE;
- causal-scope authority;
- enterprise governance; and
- ROAD release authority.

## Anti-impersonation and authority boundary

The fabric always reports:
- human credential claims: false;
- human consciousness claims: false;
- AI consciousness claims: false;
- simulated expert output is evidence: false;
- execution authority: false; and
- release authority: false.

A reasoning lens may sharpen analysis. It cannot manufacture facts, licenses, evidence, production authority, legal authority, or ROAD PASS.
