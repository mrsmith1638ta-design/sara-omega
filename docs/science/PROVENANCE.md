# Provenance Classes

SARA science calculations distinguish documented ancient methods, historically compatible reconstruction, modern engineering derivation, established physics, documented technology, engineering models, experimental technology, and simulation/hypothesis. Numerical agreement alone never promotes a reconstruction into historical fact.

## Certainty Monotonicity

The High-Level Truth Gate applies the rule: **certainty may only move downward unless stronger independent evidence is explicitly introduced.** Provenance therefore establishes a ceiling, not a floor. An engineering model cannot become established physics merely because multiple providers agree; an experimental technology cannot become deployed technology without independent deployment evidence; a historically compatible reconstruction cannot become documented ancient practice without direct historical evidence.

Every truth-gated claim carries an applicability scope and universality status. `FAMILY_LEVEL`, `ARCHITECTURE_SPECIFIC`, `CONFIGURATION_SPECIFIC`, `EXPERIMENTAL_OBSERVATION`, `HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION`, and `UNKNOWN` claims cannot be emitted as universal facts. Missing material dependencies force `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` or lower certainty.

Numerical outputs must preserve input provenance, units, assumptions, limitations, and validation status. Illustrative defaults are never user-specific facts. Time-sensitive claims marked stale or lacking current evidence are not allowed to remain verified.
