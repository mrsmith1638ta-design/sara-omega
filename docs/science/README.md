# SARA Mathematical & Physical Sciences Fabric

Implementation follows the approved design and plan under `docs/superpowers/`. Science engines are advisory specialists inside the mandatory OMEGA Council. They do not receive execution authority and must preserve provenance labels separating ancient documentation, reconstruction, modern engineering derivation, established physics, documented technology, experimental technology, and simulation/hypothesis.

## High-Level Truth Gate

All science specialist analyses are normalized through the High-Level Truth Gate before Council synthesis. The gate enforces a fail-closed certainty rule: a claim may not be stated with greater certainty or universality than its provenance, evidence, applicability scope, dependency conditions, and validation status justify.

Architecture- and configuration-dependent engineering behavior uses structured states such as `REQUIRED`, `NOT_REQUIRED`, `SYSTEM_DEPENDENT`, `UNKNOWN`, and `NOT_APPLICABLE` instead of misleading universal booleans. EDS and HTS control, low-speed, and restoring-behavior claims therefore remain system-dependent unless a specific design and sufficient supporting evidence are supplied.

The final candidate synthesis is checked again before signing. If final prose promotes a scoped claim into universal wording, SARA requests one corrected synthesis; if that still exceeds the truth ceiling, SARA returns a governed `SYSTEM_DEPENDENT / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` result rather than signing an overgeneralized fact.
